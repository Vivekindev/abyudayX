# 📚 Resources — GitHub, videos, books, blogs

Every link below was checked when this guide was built. Each lesson also has its own **Watch** and **Read deeper** boxes. This page is the master list.

## ⭐ Start with these 5 (all free)

1. **[System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer)** (donnemartin). The classic free reference, with concepts, Anki cards and solved designs.
2. **[Hello Interview — System Design in a Hurry](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/in-a-hurry/introduction)**. Modern, interview-focused, with excellent free problem breakdowns (Uber, WhatsApp, Ticketmaster, Dropbox…).
3. **[ByteByteGo system-design-101](https://github.com/ByteByteGoHq/system-design-101)**. Visual one-page explanations of almost every concept.
4. **[Awesome Low-Level Design](https://github.com/ashishps1/awesome-low-level-design)**. LLD problems and patterns, with code.
5. **[Awesome System Design Resources](https://github.com/ashishps1/awesome-system-design-resources)**. A curated map of concepts, articles and interview problems.

## 📖 Books (in priority order)

1. **System Design Interview Vol 1 & 2**, Alex Xu. Interview-shaped chapters. Vol 2 has payment, wallet, stock exchange and hotel designs, which are very relevant for banks.
2. **[Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net)**, Martin Kleppmann. The depth book. Chapters 5–9 are a must.
3. **Understanding Distributed Systems**, Roberto Vitillo. Gentler than DDIA.
4. **Head First Design Patterns**. The most readable introduction to patterns for LLD.
5. **Java Concurrency in Practice**, Brian Goetz. For Morgan Stanley / JPMorgan-style Java depth.
